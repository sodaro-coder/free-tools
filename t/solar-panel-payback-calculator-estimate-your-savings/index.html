How it works

Formula: Payback years = Panel cost ÷ Annual savings

Annual savings = Usage × Rate × 0.15 (typical production after losses)

FAQ

1. What affects payback time? Cost of panels, local electricity rates, and energy production.

2. How accurate is this estimate? It assumes typical production; actual results vary by location and installation.

3. Should I factor in incentives? Yes, subtract any tax credits or rebates from the panel cost.
