How It Works

Baker's percentages express every ingredient as a percentage of the total flour weight, which is always set to 100%. This tool also accounts for the water and flour contained inside your sourdough starter.

Formulas:

  • Ingredient % = (Ingredient Weight / Total Flour Weight) × 100
  • Total Actual Flour = Main Flour + (Starter Weight × [Starter Flour %])
  • Total Actual Water = Main Water + (Starter Weight × [Starter Water %])
  • Dough Hydration % = (Total Actual Water / Total Actual Flour) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

What is dough hydration?

Hydration is the ratio of water to flour in your bread dough. Higher hydration yields a more open, airy crumb with a blistered crust, while lower hydration creates a denser loaf that is easier to shape.

Why include the starter hydration?

Sourdough starter is made of both flour and water (usually a 100% hydration mix of equal parts flour and water). To get an accurate measurement of your total dough hydration, you must account for the water and flour coming from the starter.

How much salt should I use?

Most artisan bread recipes use between 1.8% and 2.2% salt relative to the total flour weight for optimal flavor and gluten structure.
