How it works

The calculator uses the formula:

Late Fee = Invoice Amount × (Daily Rate / 100) × Days Overdue

FAQ

  1. What if I don’t know the exact due date? You can still enter the number of days overdue directly.
  2. Can I use a weekly or monthly rate? Convert it to a daily percentage (e.g., 15% monthly ≈ 0.5% daily).
  3. Is tax included in the late fee? The calculator works on the gross invoice amount; add any tax separately if needed.
