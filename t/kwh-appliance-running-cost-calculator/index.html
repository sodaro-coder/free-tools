How it works

Cost = (Power W ÷ 1000) × Hours × Rate ($/kWh)

FAQ

  1. What does “Power (W)” mean? It is the appliance’s rated wattage, usually found on the label.
  2. Why divide by 1000? Because 1 kW = 1000 W, and rates are per kilowatt‑hour.
  3. Can I use this for monthly cost? Multiply the daily result by the number of days in the month.
