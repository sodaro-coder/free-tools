How it works

This tool uses a simple proportional scaling formula: Scaled Amount = Original Amount × (Target Servings ÷ Original Servings). When converting units, it first translates your ingredient into a base metric unit (grams for weight, milliliters for volume) before converting to your selected target unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scale a recipe for a different number of people?
Enter your recipe's default serving size, the amount of a specific ingredient, and your desired number of servings. The tool updates instantly.
Can I convert between cups and grams?
Yes, choose cups as your original unit and grams as your target unit. Note that volumetric to weight conversions are estimated based on standard water density.
What happens if I enter zero or negative numbers?
The calculator requires positive values greater than zero and will prompt you to enter valid numbers if fields are empty or invalid.
