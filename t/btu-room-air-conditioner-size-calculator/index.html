How it works

The calculator uses the formula:

BTU = (Length × Width × Height) × 4.5 × SunlightFactor + (Occupants × 600)

FAQ

  1. What does BTU mean? BTU stands for British Thermal Unit, a measure of cooling capacity.
  2. Why is sunlight factor needed? Rooms with more sun exposure need extra cooling power.
  3. Can I use the result for multiple rooms? Calculate each room separately and add the BTU values together.
