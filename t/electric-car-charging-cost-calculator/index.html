How it works

Cost per mile = (rate in $/kWh) × (efficiency in kWh/mile)
Cost per full charge = rate × capacity
Example: 13¢/kWh × 0.3 kWh/mile = 3.9¢/mile

2. Does this include home charger costs? No, only electricity usage.
