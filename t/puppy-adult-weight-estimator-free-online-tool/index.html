How it works

The estimator uses a simple growth factor that varies by breed size. The formula applied is:

adultWeight = puppyWeight × (1 + (ageWeeks / 12) × sizeFactor)

sizeFactor = 0.5 for small, 0.7 for medium, 1.0 for large breeds.

FAQ

  1. Why is the estimate only approximate? Growth rates differ between individual dogs and breeds; this tool gives a quick ball‑park figure.
  2. Can I use it for puppies older than one year? The calculator is intended for puppies up to 12 months (≈52 weeks). Beyond that, weight changes slow dramatically.
  3. Do I need to know the exact breed? No. Selecting the size category (small, medium, large) provides a reasonable approximation for mixed‑breed dogs.
