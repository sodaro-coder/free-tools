How it works

This tool uses standard cold process soap formulation math to determine the exact ingredients needed:

  • Lye (Sodium Hydroxide): Calculated by multiplying the total oil weight by the saponification value, then reducing it by the superfat percentage.
  • Superfat: Leaves a portion of the oils un-saponified for extra skin conditioning. Formula: Lye = Oils × SAP × (1 - Superfat / 100)
  • Water: Determined by taking the specified percentage of the total oil weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a superfat?
Superfatting means using slightly less lye than needed to saponify all the oils, leaving free oils in the finished soap for added moisture.
Can I use this for potassium hydroxide?
No, this specific tool calculates strictly for sodium hydroxide (hard bar soap), not potassium hydroxide (liquid soap).
Why measure ingredients in weight?
Weighing ingredients in grams ensures high accuracy and safety, which is essential for successful batch formulation.
