How it works

Total revenue = Sale price + Shipping charged to buyer

Total fees = Listing fee + (Total revenue × Transaction fee %) + (Total revenue × Payment processing % + Payment processing fixed) + (Total revenue × Offsite Ads fee %)

Total costs = COGS + Your shipping cost + Total fees

Profit = Total revenue − Total costs

Margin = (Profit ÷ Total revenue) × 100%

FAQ

Does this include the regulatory operating fee?
No. Some countries add a regulatory operating fee (e.g., UK, France, Spain). Add it manually to your costs if it applies.
What if I use Etsy Payments vs. PayPal?
The payment processing fields cover the typical Etsy Payments rate (3% + $0.25 US). Adjust the percentage and fixed fee to match your processor.
Why is my margin lower than expected?
Check that you included your actual shipping cost, not just the amount charged. Also verify the Offsite Ads fee — sales from offsite ads incur 12–15% if you’re opted in.
