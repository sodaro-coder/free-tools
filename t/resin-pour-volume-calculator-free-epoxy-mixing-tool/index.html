How it works

This tool calculates the total volume of your pour in cubic inches, converts it to fluid ounces, and then divides the total based on your specific product mixing ratio.

Formulas used:

  • Rectangle Volume = Length × Width × Depth
  • Cylinder Volume = π × (Diameter / 2)² × Depth
  • 1 Cubic Inch ≈ 0.554 Fluid Ounces

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I mix extra resin?

Yes, it is generally recommended to mix about 5 to 10 percent extra to account for liquid left clinging to mixing cups and stirring sticks.

How do I measure the depth accurately?

Measure from the bottom of your mold up to the height you want the cured surface to reach. Always check your specific product guidelines for maximum pour depth limits.

Why does the ratio matter?

Epoxy requires precise chemical ratios to cure properly. A 1:1 ratio uses equal amounts of both liquids, while a 2:1 ratio requires twice as much resin as hardener by volume.
