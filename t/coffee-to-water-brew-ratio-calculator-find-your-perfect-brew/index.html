How it works

The calculator uses the simple formula:

Water (ml) = Coffee (g) × Ratio

FAQ

  1. What does the ratio mean? It represents how many millilitres of water you use per gram of coffee.
  2. Can I change the ratio? Yes—different brew methods use different ratios (e.g., 15 for espresso, 18 for pour‑over).
  3. Why is the water result in millilitres? Millilitres give a precise volume for most home brewing equipment.
