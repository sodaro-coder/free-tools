How It Works

This tool estimates your dog's daily caloric needs using the Resting Energy Requirement (RER) formula, then adjusts for age and activity level.

Formula: Daily Calories = (70 × (Weight in lbs ÷ 2.2)^0.75) × Activity Factor

  • Puppies: 2.0-3.0x RER (we use 2.5x average)
  • Adults: 1.4-1.6x RER (we use 1.5x average)
  • Seniors: 1.2-1.4x RER (we use 1.3x average)
  • Activity Adjustment: Low = 0.9x, Moderate = 1.0x, High = 1.2x

Finally, we divide the total daily calories by the calories per cup in your specific food to get the number of cups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this calculator?

This tool provides a general estimate based on standard veterinary guidelines. Individual dogs may need more or less depending on breed, metabolism, health conditions, and specific food digestibility. Always monitor your dog's body condition and consult your veterinarian for personalized feeding advice.

How should I divide the daily amount?

For most adult dogs, divide the total daily cups into 2 equal meals. Puppies under 6 months should typically eat 3-4 smaller meals. Adjust the number of meals based on your dog's age and appetite, but keep the total daily amount the same.

What if my dog is overweight or underweight?

If your dog is overweight, start with the calculated amount and reduce by 20-30% while increasing activity. If underweight, start with the calculated amount and increase by 10-20% gradually. Monitor weight weekly and adjust as needed. Your vet can help determine the ideal target weight.
