How it works

Volume is calculated as Length × Width × Average Depth × 7.48 (gallons per cubic foot). The chlorine dose is determined by the volume and your target parts per million (ppm), assuming a standard liquid concentration of 10% available chlorine.

FAQ

1. Why is 7.48 used? It is the standard conversion factor for cubic feet to US gallons.

2. Is this dose safe? Always follow local safety guidelines and test your water before adding chemicals.

3. Can I use this for salt systems? This calculator is specifically for liquid chlorine additions; salt systems function differently.
