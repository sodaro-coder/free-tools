How it works

This tool uses geometric formulas to calculate total water volume and applies a standard guideline for safe fish capacity.

  • Rectangular Volume: Length × Width × Height ÷ 231 = US Gallons
  • Bowfront Volume: (Length × Width × Height × 0.85) ÷ 231 = US Gallons (accounts for curved front glass)
  • Cylindrical Volume: (π × Radius² × Height) ÷ 231 = US Gallons
  • Stocking Limit: Generally calculated as 1 inch of adult fish per gallon of water, modified by your filtration quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I measure the inside or outside of the tank?
Measure the inside dimensions for the most accurate water volume. If measuring the outside, subtract about 1 inch from each dimension to account for glass thickness and empty space at the top.
Is the 1 inch per gallon rule exact?
No, it is a general baseline for small community fish. Body shape, swimming activity, biological waste output, and adult fish size matter more than length alone.
How does filtration affect stocking capacity?
Stronger biological filtration and live plants process waste faster, allowing for a slightly higher fish capacity while maintaining stable water parameters.
