Candle Wax & Fragrance Oil Calculator – Quick Ratio Tool





How it works

Fragrance = Wax × (Load ÷ 100) (grams)
If you need the volume, divide the grams by the oil density (g/ml).

FAQ

  1. What is “fragrance load”? The percentage of the total wax weight you want to be fragrance oil.
  2. Why does density matter? Different oils weigh slightly different amounts per milliliter; using the correct density gives an accurate volume.
  3. Can I use ounces? Convert ounces to grams (1 oz ≈ 28.35 g) and enter the gram value.
