Raised Bed Soil Calculator

How it works

The formula for calculating soil volume is simple:

Volume = Length × Width × Depth

This gives you the total cubic feet of soil needed to fill your raised bed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bags of soil do I need?

Most bags of soil contain 1-2 cubic feet. Divide your total cubic feet by the bag size to determine how many bags to purchase.

Should I add amendments to my soil?

Yes, most raised beds benefit from a mix of soil, compost, and other amendments. Consider adding 20-30% compost to your soil mix.

What depth is best for raised beds?

Most vegetables grow well with 12-18 inches of soil depth. Root vegetables may need deeper soil (18-24 inches).
